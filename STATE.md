For a person with a current `keepDetails` consent the period is five years, so on the first night after `until` plus three years the place is gone, the last activity falls back, and the person can be anonymised before `until` plus five years. The old view did the same. The fix is a decision: keep the place's row as long as it counts as activity, or keep the place's end in a column that outlives the row. Nobody has decided; nothing is built.
