# Low-use features leave the core for Google Workspace

Surveys, social events, team applications and certificate requests are not rebuilt. They move to Google Forms and Google Calendar. Sponsor work, marketing and team wikis stay in Notion or Google Drive. The core keeps only the work that needs fixed rules: admission, recruitment, placement, teams, expense claims and accounts. Data moves one way, from the core to the other tools.

In the 2024 backup, the last survey answer is from 2022-12-05, the last article from 2018, and there are 10 social events, 15 team applications and 6 certificate requests in total. These features carry about a quarter of the legacy model but few of its rules (22 of 331 rules leave with them).

## Consequences

- The core shrinks from 48 legacy tables to 12 entities, plus the interview form construct.
- Phase 5 of the modernization brief no longer rebuilds surveys.
- A certificate is generated from placement history; the request form is a Google Form.
